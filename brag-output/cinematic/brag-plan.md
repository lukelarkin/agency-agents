# Brag Plan: Larkin Systems — The moat is underneath (cinematic)

## What is this app?
Larkin Systems builds AI operations systems for mid-market operators. This film dramatizes one case study from the site: the four-system stack under a custom home builder's Meta lead-gen launch in the Treasure Valley.

## The angle
The site's thesis is "Anyone can run ads. The moat is underneath." So the camera goes underneath. The ad is the surface; the film dives through the four systems beneath it, one layer at a time, and ends by pulling back to reveal the whole stack the campaign rides on.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"ANYONE CAN RUN ADS." slams in at full frame on a bell hit. It shrinks to a kicker as a skeleton Meta ad card rises into frame. "THE MOAT IS UNDERNEATH." rises out of a line mask, and the camera dives through the ad.

## Key moments (the middle)
- 01 The dirt: a stylized county map. The road draws, a 2.3-mile sewer main extension draws in copper along it, six parcels outline in, and three light up and get ranked 01 / 02 / 03 on the beat grid. "2WK" slams in: first contact placed before the hearing.
- 02 The permit: five knowledge layers waterfall in; "Per-reviewer profiles" is the hot one, and a copper rule draws to "THE MOAT". Three cited outputs stack on the right. "LOADED AGAINST TWO COUNTY CODES."
- 03 The campaign, audited: fourteen deliverable cards fly one by one through a copper QA gate and stack up checked; the ninth stops at the gate on a bell hit: "Held at the gate. Meta Housing Special Ad Category compliance gap." A counter runs to 12 numerical conflicts reconciled. "ALL RECONCILED PRE-LAUNCH."
- 04 The lead: a 300px timer counts 0:00 to 0:60, "TEXT SENT · IN THE BUILDER'S VOICE" stamps in, the escalation line draws (0:01 text, 0:15 text, 0:30 email, 2:00 call), "6-HOUR WINDOW" is struck through and "UNDER 5 MINUTES." slams in, then "8×" conversion lift.

## Outro / punchline
A 3D pull-back: the camera tilts and rises to reveal all four layers and the surface ad as a stack of plates in perspective. The stack dims and "LARKIN SYSTEMS / Operations systems that ship. / larkin.systems" lands on the strongest cue.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only. The centerpiece is the case study's four systems, each shown doing its thing (map scoring, layer stack, QA gate, responder timer), not the site's sections.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: a descent; the camera is the argument
- Interpretation: all-caps heavy type, one zoom-through transition language throughout (old scene flies past the lens, new arrives from behind), hard slams on strong cues, a persistent depth meter top-right that ticks down a level on every dive, one true 3D move at the end.

- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; RMS/bass drive the copper glows' opacity and scale and the depth meter's indicator glow
