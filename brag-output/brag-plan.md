# Brag Plan: Larkin Systems

## What is this app?
A single-page agency site (larkin.systems) that sells AI operations systems to mid-market operators — and the whole site is built to prove it is "not another AI demo": real numbers, real systems in production, one CTA.

## The angle
The site's thesis is restraint. Every competitor's launch video is loud; this one is quiet, precise, and lets the product's own copy do the bragging. The video is a 24-second walk down the actual page — hero, proof strip, production tiles, the insight line, the CTA — rebuilt at video scale in the site's own near-black + copper system. The brag is: the site already says everything, we just put it in motion.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Near-black frame, a copper glow breathing with the music. The eyebrow LARKIN SYSTEMS settles. Then the real hero line rises out of a line mask: **"Not another AI demo."** One soft impact. That sentence is the hook — it is the site's own first line.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The proof strip: four Geist Mono numbers counting up in sequence — 12+ production systems shipped, 60+ unit property portfolio deployed, 9 verticals with shipped work, 1 operator end-to-end — on the site's hairline-divided surface band.
- "Running in production right now." then three work tiles arriving one by one with their real vertical/geography tags, the copper "In production" chip, and trimmed real tile copy.
- The insight line: hairline draws, "Anyone can run ads." then "The moat is underneath." with "moat" in copper and the underline drawing in.

## Outro / punchline
"No pitch deck. No proposal template. Just the call." one line per beat, then the copper **Book the diagnostic** button lands. Cut to the lockup: Larkin Systems · Built in Idaho · Deployed everywhere · larkin.systems.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only. The site has no app; the flow is the page itself (hero → proof → work → insight → CTA). The centerpiece scenes recreate the proof strip and the work tiles, which are the two most UI-like sections.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film — "restraint is the moat"
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, slow crossfade-feel cuts over a persistent background, nothing aggressive. Motion vocabulary is borrowed from the site itself: line-mask headline rises, hairline draws, fade-in-up reveals, drifting copper glows.
